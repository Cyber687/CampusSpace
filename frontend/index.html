Active QR registrations are compared with canteen capacity. They estimate participation, not the exact physical headcount or queue time.

How to use CampusSpace

  • Find a Room: Use the Classroom Finder. Green means recently verified.
  • Book a Room: Click "Book 1hr" to reserve a space.
  • Report Faults: Go to Fault Reports, pick a location, and submit a ticket.
  • Canteen: Use Scan & Done to check in and see crowd levels.
